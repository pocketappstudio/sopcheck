# SOPCheck: Gumroad listing copy (draft)

**Name:** SOPCheck: SOP-to-checklist tool (monthly membership)

**Short description:** Turn your standard operating procedures into reviewed, role-based technician checklists, with an automated safety check, versioning and an audit log.

**Description**

SOPCheck is an informational tool that turns the SOP documents you upload into draft checklists for your technicians.

- AI-assisted extraction of tasks, roles, sites, timeframes and verification steps.
- An automated safety check (heuristic, not a guarantee) that flags safety-related sentences that may be missing from a checklist.
- A manager review step: nothing reaches technicians until a manager reviews and publishes it.
- Technician view with sign-offs, photos and readings; versioning, audit log and data export.

**Important**

- SOPCheck is an informational tool. **AI can be wrong**, including on safety-critical steps. Always verify every checklist against your official SOP before use.
- **No compliance guarantee.** SOPCheck does not certify, guarantee or verify compliance with any law, standard or regulation, and it is not professional, engineering, safety, medical or legal advice. Your organization is responsible for its workers, sites and decisions.
- For business use by adults 18+. Document text is processed by an AI service (OpenAI). Upload only documents you have the right to share.
- Free 14-day trial inside the app, no card needed. Monthly memberships are billed and refunded through Gumroad only. After purchase, paste your Gumroad license key in the app under Manager → Billing.
- Support, billing questions and refunds go through Gumroad messaging.

**Preview:** try the free demo at https://pocketappstudio.github.io/sopcheck/ (sample data only; do not upload confidential documents).
