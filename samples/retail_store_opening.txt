FreshMart Stores — Store Opening Procedure (OPS-03)

Opening Manager responsibilities — complete before 7:00 AM

Back Office
1. Disarm the alarm system and log the disarm time.
2. Count the opening cash drawers; each drawer must contain exactly $150.00.
3. Sign the cash count sheet.

Sales Floor — Opening
4. Walk the sales floor and check aisles for spills, trip hazards, or blocked fire exits. Fire exits must remain clear per local fire code.
5. Verify the promotional endcap displays match this week's planogram and take a photo of each endcap.

Refrigerated Section — Opening, then Every 2 hours
6. Associate on Duty: Read and record the cooler temperature. Coolers must be at or below 41 F; if above 41 F, remove product and notify the Opening Manager.

Front Entrance — Opening
7. Unlock the front doors and turn on the OPEN sign.
