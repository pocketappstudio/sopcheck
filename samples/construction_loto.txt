SOP-ELEC-014: Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) for Electrical Panel Maintenance
Company: Apex Builders Inc.   Revision: C   Regulatory basis: OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147, NFPA 70E

1. PURPOSE
This procedure ensures hazardous energy is controlled before servicing 480V distribution panels.

2. RESPONSIBILITIES
The Authorized Electrician performs all isolation steps. The Site Safety Officer verifies isolation for group lockouts.

3. PRE-TASK (Main Electrical Room)
3.1 Authorized Electrician: Review the one-line diagram and identify all energy sources feeding Panel DP-2.
3.2 Don PPE: Class 0 insulated gloves, arc-rated face shield, and arc flash suit rated minimum 8 cal/cm2.
WARNING: Never work on energized equipment above 50 volts without an approved energized work permit.

4. ISOLATION (Main Electrical Room)
4.1 Notify all affected employees that the panel will be shut down.
4.2 Open the main breaker and apply your personal lock and danger tag to the breaker handle.
4.3 Verify zero energy using a CAT III rated voltmeter. Record voltage reading; it must read less than 1 volt on all phases.
4.4 Site Safety Officer: Independently verify isolation and co-sign the lockout log.
DO NOT proceed until both signatures are recorded.

5. RESTORATION (Post-Task)
5.1 Remove all tools and confirm all personnel are clear of the panel.
5.2 Remove your lock and tag and re-energize the panel.
5.3 Photograph the closed panel with the dead-front cover installed.
5.4 Sign the LOTO completion record.
